# Extraction fundamentals (distilled)
source: https://example.org/extraction-primer
retrieved: 2026-07-14

Key claims
- Roasted coffee is ~28-30% water-soluble by mass; the rest is insoluble
  cellulose. Extraction yield is the share of that soluble mass dissolved.
- The widely used target band is 18-22% yield; below tastes sour/grassy,
  above tastes bitter/astringent.
- Strength (TDS, typically 8-12% for espresso) is a concentration and is
  set mainly by brew ratio. Yield and strength are independent.

Numbers worth quoting
- 1:2 in 25-30s is the conventional espresso starting point.
- Doubling contact time does not double yield — dissolution decelerates
  as the easily-soluble fraction is depleted.
