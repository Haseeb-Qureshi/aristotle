# Diagnostic taste chart (distilled)
source: https://example.org/taste-diagnosis
retrieved: 2026-07-14

Key claims
- Dissolution order: acids first, sugars mid, bitter phenolics last.
  Taste therefore acts as a clock for where extraction stopped.
- Sour + fast shot -> grind finer. Bitter + slow shot -> grind coarser.
- Sour AND bitter together in one cup is the channeling signature: a
  low-resistance path over-extracts while the rest under-extracts.

Caution
- Very dark roasts are bitter regardless of yield; taste diagnosis
  assumes a medium roast baseline.
