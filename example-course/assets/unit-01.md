## concept: extraction-yield
- quiz: Two shots use the same dose. One runs 18s, one runs 32s. Which has the higher extraction yield, and why? | a: the 32s shot — longer contact dissolves a larger fraction of the bean's solubles, independent of how strong it tastes | distractor: M1
- quiz: A shot tastes intensely strong but thin and sour. Is it highly extracted? | a: no — strength is high (little water) while extraction is low; the sourness is the tell | distractor: M1
- example: worked | Same 18g dose, two shots. Shot A: 25g out in 20s, tastes sharp and citric. Shot B: 40g out in 32s, tastes fuller, slightly drying. B pulled more mass out of the bean — higher yield — even though A tastes more intense per sip, because A has less water carrying it.

## concept: strength-vs-extraction
- quiz: You add 30g of hot water to a finished espresso. What happens to strength, and what happens to extraction yield? | a: strength falls (same solids, more water); extraction yield is unchanged — it was fixed the moment the shot stopped | distractor: M2
- quiz: Name the two independent axes and one variable that moves each. | a: strength (moved by brew ratio) and extraction (moved by grind and contact time) | distractor: M1
- example: worked | Picture a 2x2. Weak and under-extracted: sour and watery — grind finer AND tighten the ratio. Strong and over-extracted: bitter and syrupy — grind coarser AND open the ratio. Diagnosing needs both axes; a single "strong/weak" scale cannot tell those two apart.

## concept: grind-size
- quiz: Why is grind the first thing to change when a shot runs too fast? | a: grind sets flow resistance; finer means slower flow, longer contact, higher yield | distractor: M1
- quiz: Your shot runs 15s and tastes sour. Grind finer or coarser? | a: finer — raise resistance, extend contact time, raise extraction | distractor: M1
- example: worked | Think of the puck as a filter, not a flavour ingredient. Finer particles pack tighter, water fights through more slowly, contact time rises, yield rises. Grind is a resistance dial whose effect on taste is entirely downstream.

## rubric: strength-vs-extraction
- claim: strength is dissolved solids per unit water, set largely by brew ratio
- claim: extraction yield is the fraction of the bean's solubles dissolved, set by grind and contact time
- claim: the two vary independently — a shot can be strong and under-extracted, or weak and over-extracted
- avoid: M1
- avoid: M2
