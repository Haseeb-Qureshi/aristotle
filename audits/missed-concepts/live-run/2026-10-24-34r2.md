## taught
- benchmark-construction: eight-attempt score measures model plus repeated attempts and selection; fair comparison matches cases, attempts and checker and includes extra execution cost
- workload-shapes: rough memory anchors of 2 bytes per parameter for serving weights versus 16-20 with training gradients and optimizer state; training arithmetic roughly three times forward-only; synchronous training can stall on one failed worker until recovery while serving reroutes independent requests; prefill uses parallel compute while sequential decode moves weights and cached context through memory, motivating specialized hardware; RL generation uses inference capacity, checking often uses CPUs and updates use tightly connected training GPUs; final clarification that rollouts often dominate RL elapsed runtime but the actual mix must be measured; older hardware reuse requires competitive cost rather than merely model fit
